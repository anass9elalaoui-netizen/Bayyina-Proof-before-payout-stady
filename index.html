01 · The problem

Every claim photo is now an attack surface

Most motor and home claims are now settled from photos taken by the customer, not by an expert on site. It is faster and cheaper. It also means the insurer pays on evidence it never saw being created.

In 2026 image generators produce photorealistic dents, cracked windscreens and flooded floors in seconds. Edited and recycled photos were already a problem; generated ones make it a scale problem.

03 · Insight

Detection is a race. Proof is not.

Every new image model makes yesterday's fake detector weaker. A cryptographic signature made at the moment of capture does not get weaker.

Today insurers defend themselves after the upload: more angles, original files by post, live video calls, AI-image detectors. Each step adds cost or friction, and detectors age.

The better order is to prove what can be proven at capture, check only what cannot, and give the AI the trusted evidence plus the policy. Large vendors each own one of these pieces. Mid-size insurers, brokers and claims hubs cannot afford to stitch three of them together, and many of their customers write in more than one language.

Step 1

Prove at capture

The app hashes and signs each photo on the device, tied to a one-time code for this claim. Any later edit breaks the signature.

Step 2

Check the rest

Uploads without a signature go through metadata checks, compression analysis, an AI-image detector and a search for photos reused from other claims.

Step 3

Reason, then decide

An AI agent reads trusted photos, the policy and the customer's story, cites the clauses it used, and a named handler makes the final call.

05 · Architecture

A modular core with a human at the end

Exhibit D · Containers and flow
Decision 1

Proof beats detection

A valid signature outweighs any detector score. Detection only covers evidence that was not proven.

Decision 2

Rules after the model

The final recommendation comes from readable, versioned rules, so behaviour stays predictable and auditable.

Decision 3

A human always decides

No claim is paid or rejected automatically. Overrides of the AI are logged and reviewed.
